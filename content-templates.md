## General Site-Wide Guidelines (All Templates)

**SEO Basics:**
- Target one primary keyword per post (use in H1, first 100 words, meta description)
- Use 2-3 secondary keywords naturally throughout
- Include keyword in at least one H2
- Alt text on all images must include relevant keywords
- Internal linking: minimum 5 links per post, spread throughout

**Affiliate Strategy (Amazon hiddencamerastv-20):**
- Every product mention = affiliate link (no exceptions for reviewed products)
- Comparison tables: each product links to Amazon
- "Recommended Products" sections in tutorials = 3 tiers (budget/mid/premium)
- Disclose affiliate relationship in first H2 section and in footer
- Price check: update prices quarterly (or note "prices as of [date]")

**Content Structure:**
- Hook in first paragraph — address the reader's specific need
- Use comparison tables for any product comparison
- Break up text every 200-300 words with subheadings, images, or lists
- FAQ sections capture featured snippet opportunities
- Include "Last Updated: [DATE]" at top of all review/comparison posts

**Trust Signals:**
- Include "We tested this" language wherever possible
- Show testing methodology (even if informal)
- Include pros AND cons for every product (honesty = trust = clicks)
- Mention alternatives if a product isn't right for everyone
- Include real-world use scenarios, not just specs

**Monetization Hooks:**
- Amazon affiliate links (hiddencamerastv-20) on every product
- Email capture: free security checklist or camera buying guide
- "Read Next" internal links to keep users browsing
- Social proof: "As featured on [publication]" if applicable
