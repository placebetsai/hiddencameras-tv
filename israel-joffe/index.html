Founder · Former firefighter · Builder

Israel Joffe

Built HiddenCameras.tv because home-security review sites are the most affiliate-corrupted vertical on the English-speaking web, and someone who’s been through actual fire scenes has a reason to push back.

The angle

The home-security review industry is broken. Type “best wireless security camera 2026” into Google and the entire first page is affiliate sites that earn $30-$70 per Amazon click and have never seen most of the products in person. Half are AI-generated. The other half are press-list curated — manufacturers send the product, the “reviewer” spends 20 minutes setting it up, writes a glowing piece, keeps the unit. Nobody talks about the false-positive rate at 2 AM. Nobody talks about the cloud subscription doubling year-over-year.

Israel Joffe built HiddenCameras.tv as a counterweight. He’s a former NYC firefighter (Lawrence-Cedarhurst Fire Department, served through Hurricane Sandy until back injuries forced an exit), an IT specialist by training (network and systems administration), and someone who has stood in actual house fires looking at melted Ring doorbells and Nest cams. The site reviews home-security gear with the question affiliate sites won’t answer: does this thing actually catch a problem at 2 AM, and what happens when it doesn’t?

Where he’s from

Born and raised in New York City. Spent most of his life there. Served LCFD through Sandy. Moved to Washington DC in 2016. Settled in the Palm Beach area in 2021, where Israel Joffe now operates a portfolio of niche web properties from a home office in Boca Raton, Florida. The federation: placebets.ai, scooter.exchange, diamonds.forsale, ihatecollege.com, spanishtvshows.com.
